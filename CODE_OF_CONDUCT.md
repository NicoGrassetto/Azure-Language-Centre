# Code of conduct

Azure Language Centre welcomes contributors and learners of all backgrounds and
experience levels. This code applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews,
and other project-managed community spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and considerate, especially with people who are
  learning.
- Give constructive feedback about ideas and code, not personal characteristics.
- Accept good-faith feedback and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Respect different perspectives, identities, abilities, and experiences.
- Protect people's privacy and obtain consent before sharing their information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexual harassment, spam,
and deliberate disruption are not welcome. Do not publish another person's
private information, credentials, or customer data.

## Reporting concerns

For a non-sensitive concern that can safely be addressed publicly, notify the
[repository owner](https://github.com/NicoGrassetto) in the relevant issue or
pull request. Share only the information needed for moderation.

For abuse or sensitive safety concerns, use the
[GitHub abuse-report form](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse?category=report-abuse&report=other&report_type=unspecified)
to contact GitHub Support privately. This is not a private project-maintainer
inbox. Do not post private details publicly or use vulnerability reports for
conduct disputes.

Report code or deployment vulnerabilities using the
[security policy](./SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may request changes, remove inappropriate content, close disruptive
conversations, or restrict participation, depending on the severity and pattern
of behavior. Serious or repeated violations may result in removal from
project-managed spaces. Retaliation against someone who raises a good-faith
concern is itself a violation.
